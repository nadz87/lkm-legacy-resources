How We Started

Founded in crisis. Built for the long run.

LKM Legacy Group was founded in 2021, at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic — a moment when hygiene stopped being a preference and became a matter of survival. As Malaysia faced unprecedented uncertainty, businesses and communities urgently needed a partner they could trust to keep spaces safe, sanitised, and operational.

What began as a small, determined team answering a national need has since grown into a trusted facility and manpower partner for some of Malaysia's most recognised brands. From disinfecting frontline facilities during lockdown to servicing multinational manufacturing plants today, our foundation has never changed: show up, do it right, never compromise on quality.

Carbon Footprint Measurement

Our Environmental Commitment

As LKM Legacy Group grows, we're committed to responsible environmental practices across every site we operate. Understanding and measuring our carbon footprint is the first step toward reducing it.

The classification of emissions into Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 is defined under the internationally recognised Greenhouse Gas Protocol, developed by the World Resources Institute and the World Business Council for Sustainable Development.

Scope 1

Direct Emissions

Direct greenhouse gas emissions from sources we own or control, including fuel combustion in company vehicles, generators, or equipment using petrol or diesel.

Scope 2

Indirect Energy Emissions

Indirect emissions from purchased electricity consumed across our operations — including our Penang headquarters and branch sites — attributed to us because the electricity is used in our day-to-day work.

Scope 3

Other Indirect Emissions

All other indirect emissions across our value chain, including business travel, employee commuting, purchased goods and services, and waste disposal from our cleaning operations.

Our Tracking Initiative. LKM Legacy Group is establishing a baseline for Scope 2 emissions by recording total electricity consumption at our Penang headquarters, to be converted into estimated carbon emissions using Malaysia's official LCOS SME Carbon Footprint Calculator (Malaysian Green Technology and Climate Change Corporation), which applies Malaysia's electricity grid emission factors. Measured results will be published here as they become available.

By monitoring this data, we aim to identify opportunities to improve energy efficiency and reduce our environmental impact — reflecting our long-term commitment to responsible environmental management as we scale.

Where We're Headed

A proven model, built to scale

As LKM Legacy Group scales, we're building a group structure that separates operations, training, and franchising — each its own entity, anchored by the same parent company. This includes a planned HRDC-registered Training Centre and LKM Legacy Franchise Sdn Bhd, licensing our brand and operating systems to partners under the Franchise Act 1998 — turning a proven operating model into a scalable, replicable business.
